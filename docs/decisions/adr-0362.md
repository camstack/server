## Context

The snapshot wrapper is polled: grids, cards and the media plane ask for a
frame on a cadence, and on a cache miss the wrapper dials the camera's
native provider (ISAPI, CGI, Baichuan…) before falling through to the
stream-broker frame grab. Every miss was followed by another dial on the
next poll. "Camera retro casa" (Hikvision on a weak link) answered
HTTP 503 to 68 native snapshot dials in one hour on 2026-09-05 — one
request per poll, on a camera already too weak to keep its streams up. The
provider caught each failure and returned `null`, so the wrapper read it as
"no native image, try the broker" and asked again two seconds later.

Putting a backoff in the Hikvision provider would have fixed one vendor.
Every vendor's native provider sits behind the same wrapper, and the polling
that causes the load is the wrapper's.

## Decision

The snapshot wrapper keeps one rest ledger (`NativeSnapshotFatigue`). A
native dial that produces no frame — thrown or answered `null` — earns the
camera a rest window (10 s, doubling per consecutive miss, capped at 5 min)
during which the native provider is NOT dialled: the request falls through
to the broker grab and the stale cache, which cost the camera nothing. The
first frame that lands ends the rest and the streak. `force` bypasses the
ledger: an operator's deliberate wake is never held back. A removed device
is forgotten with its caches. The rest is logged at info with the camera
and the time left, once per request that would have dialled.

## Consequences

A camera that cannot answer is asked less, not more, and the load on it
drops with the length of the outage. The cost is latency to notice a
recovery: after a five-minute rest the camera is retried at most once per
window until a frame lands. A battery camera answering `null` while asleep
also rests, which is the right side of the trade — a forced snapshot still
wakes it. Providers that already back off internally (none did) now get the
same treatment twice; nothing here reads a vendor's own state.

## Enforcement

`snapshot-fatigue.spec.ts` (windows, doubling, cap, reset, forget) and the
wrapper test "lets a camera that could not answer REST" in
`snapshot.addon.spec.ts` (no second dial inside the window, retry after,
`force` bypass). Review only for a new native-call site in the wrapper.
