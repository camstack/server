## Context

`TimelineSession.resolveProfile` accepted the requested tier whenever that
tier had footage ANYWHERE in the archive: `locateSegment` answers a gap
with `nearestEdgeMs`, the next covered start with no bound on distance. The
feeder, meanwhile, refuses to snap further than `SNAP_MAX_DISTANCE_MS`
(120 s). So with High deleted for a range but still recording on a new disk
— the disk-swap case the operator is about to make — every seek into the
hole resolved to `high`, the feeder refused, and the player showed
"no recording" over Mid and Low footage that existed. With High gone from
the whole archive the session fell to Mid silently under a chip that said
High. The client could not repair either: it re-sends `setProfile` only when
its own mirror disagrees, and nothing ever told the mirror the server moved.
`pickProfileWithFootage` asked the right question and had no caller.

## Decision

A tier has footage for a seek only where the feeder could snap: a segment
covering the instant, or a gap whose snap target (next start, else the last
ms behind) lies within `SNAP_MAX_DISTANCE_MS` — one constant, exported from
`playback-profile-fallback.ts` and imported by the feeder. The requested
tier is still checked first and wins on a near gap (the `low` scrub pin
does not regress); only an unreachable tier falls to the next one that can
play. The session keeps the client's REQUEST apart from the tier it serves,
so a later seek re-resolves from the request and gets High back where High
exists. Every change of the (served, requested) pair is sent to the client
as `servedProfile`; the embed moves its pin mirror to the served tier,
drops a delayed scrub upgrade into a tier just reported degraded, and
forwards the report to the host. The host draws one small letter beside
play — never a message.

## Consequences

The operator can drop un-migrated High footage: seeks into that range play
Mid, then Low, and the bar says so. A scrub that lands where Low has no
footage within two minutes but High does now plays High with the Low-only
pre-cache budgets off, where it used to show a gap; the report makes that
visible. `auto` still climbs by bandwidth only; the veto stops it climbing
into a tier the server just refused for this request.

## Enforcement

`timeline-session.spec.ts` (far gap falls to the covering tier and reports;
near gap keeps the requested tier; the report fires once per change and
again on recovery), `playback-profile-fallback.spec.ts`, the control
protocol and ui-library mirror specs, and `recorded-track-session.spec.ts`
(mirror moves, upgrade vetoed, veto lifts). Removing the reachability gate
fails two session tests (verified).
