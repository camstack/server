## Context

A consumer joining a live stream mid-GOP has nothing to decode until the
camera's next IDR, so the broker replays its retained GOP on connect. Two
independent implementations of that replay existed, and only one of them had
ever been fixed.

The WebRTC path was corrected in `paced-rtp-replay.ts`, whose header states the
failure verbatim: a synchronous burst "overruns the peer's socket/jitter buffers
— packets dropped mid-burst leave the decoder without a complete GOP, so nothing
renders until the camera's NEXT live IDR". The RTSP restreamer, four directories
away, kept the shape that document describes as broken: a nested synchronous
loop over the entire retained ring, with no pacer and no bound.

Measured at the broker's own output on 2026-08-06, three runs each:

| stream | burst | wall clock | instantaneous | steady |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 101/mid | 224 KB | 181 ms | 9.9 Mbps | 485 kbps (20×) |
| 101/high | 1.27 MB | 250 ms | 40.7 Mbps | 7.8 Mbps (5.2×) |

and over 2 277 bursts that day: span p50 1 800 ms, p90 3 954 ms, **p99 28 312 ms,
max 61 535 ms / 17 065 packets**. The only ceiling was `RTP_RING_MAX_BYTES`
(24 MB), and hitting it discarded the ring outright.

The confirmation came from the far end. A HomeKit session ending 17:43:16.545Z
reported `maxJitter` 272 324 at the 90 kHz clock = **3 030 ms**; the broker had
logged, at that session's start, `101/native:slot-3 restream prime … packets:187,
spanMs: 2996`. **The jitter the controller measured is the burst span.** The
HomeKit leg is an ffmpeg copy of the non-muted restream URL, so it takes the
whole burst and forwards the compression to iOS on its own real-time clock.

Two related asymmetries were found while tracing it. `prime` writes bypassed the
`MAX_WRITE_BUFFER` gate the live path honours, so a join could queue up to 24 MB
into one socket that refuses 2 MB of live traffic. And a burst that was too wide
had no bound at all — the muted (local decoder) path had had a live-edge gate
since the pts-backlog fix, but nothing equivalent protected anyone else.

## Decision

A join burst is **paced** through the single shared `PacedRtpReplay` — never a
second pacer, and never a synchronous loop — and it is **bounded**: over
`PRIME_MAX_SPAN_MS` (2 000 ms) of media time, or over `PRIME_MAX_PACKETS`
(2 000), it is not trimmed but **withheld**, and the session joins at the next
live keyframe access unit instead. Prime writes obey backpressure like every
other write; a refused write aborts the whole replay rather than punching a hole
in the GOP.

Trimming is not an option and the reason is structural: a window taken from the
ring's tail no longer begins at a keyframe, so it cannot be decoded. The choice
is the whole GOP or none of it. 2 000 ms sits above the observed p50 and below
the confirmed-harmful 2 996 ms, so a short-GOP camera keeps its instant picture
and the long tail is cut.

## Consequences

- A stream whose IDR interval exceeds 2 s hands its consumers no picture until
  the next keyframe — bounded by that same interval, and clean at the live edge
  rather than that many seconds in the past. The recorder loses at most one GOP
  of head at the start of a recording; it holds one long-lived RTSP session, so
  this is once per start, not per segment.
- Ordering is now explicit rather than implicit: a live packet arriving during a
  replay is appended to its tail via `enqueue`, never sent ahead of it. Anything
  that bypasses `activeReplays` in the broadcast loop reintroduces reordering.
- Audio is deliberately NOT held behind a video replay; the consumer aligns the
  tracks by RTP timestamp.
- `PacedRtpReplay` may now be aborted from inside its own `send` callback, which
  the RTSP path relies on. `drainChunk` stops the current chunk when that
  happens.
- The live-edge gate for muted sessions (`LIVE_EDGE_MAX_SPAN_MS`, 500 ms) is
  unchanged and remains a separate, tighter rule.

## Enforcement

`packages/addon-pipeline/src/stream-broker/__tests__/rtsp-prime-pacing.spec.ts`
pins the properties a "did every packet arrive?" test would miss: that no single
synchronous turn emits the whole ring, that total order is burst-then-live, that
a burst wider than the bound is not replayed at all, and that a backlogged
socket stops the burst instead of absorbing it. Each was confirmed to fail when
its fix is removed. No guard script — a second pacer would be caught in review,
by the module doc on `paced-rtp-replay.ts`.
