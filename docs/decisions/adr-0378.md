## Context

Camera 101, the rule "Animale su Aiuola". The reference NVR notifies the dog; CamStack
does not. D372 built the native second pass, D377 stopped the confirmation gate
from re-asking the question on worse pixels and stopped a birth being judged
twice. The recovery pass demonstrably works — 2026-09-07 06:59:36–38, four
passes in 1.9 s, `recovered=1 classes=["animal"]` on every one, i.e. AT the
`RECOVERY_MIN_INTERVAL_MS` (500 ms) cadence cap. Still no notification.

Over two hours the operator measured five `animal` events on 101 and the dog is
outside the Aiuola polygon in every one of them; the tracks die young
(`durationMs=176 positions=3`, `durationMs=593 positions=2`,
`durationMs=4653 positions=6`). The dog crosses the field from the left and is
lost before it reaches the flowerbed. The zone stamping itself was checked
against the real polygon and is correct (2 disagreements in 72 events, both a
person at exactly the 10% overlap bar).

The diagnostics D377 shipped answered it within an hour of going live. Over the
35 minutes 07:43–08:18, `tracker: detection dropped — no track was born from it`
fired **43 times, 11 of them on a box carrying `nativeRecovery`**:

```
07:51:33  reason=cross-class-absorbed detClass=animal detScore=0.833
          nativeRecoveryScore=0.833 bbox=198x186 againstTrackClass=person
07:56:37  reason=occlusion         detClass=animal detScore=0.5143
          nativeRecoveryScore=0.5143 bbox=105x107 threshold=0.6
08:16:24  reason=cross-class-absorbed detClass=animal detScore=0.8446
          nativeRecoveryScore=0.8446 bbox=277x177 againstTrackClass=person
```

Two branches, both about the COARSE pass, both applied to a native measurement:

1. **Association.** `classGroupAssoc` collapses {person, animal} into ONE
   association group so a bending person read as `animal` for a frame re-matches
   the existing person track instead of minting a concurrent id. A recovered dog
   box near its owner is reached by the same gate — through the rescue pass's
   `rescueIouThreshold` (0.1) as easily as through `iouThreshold` (0.3) — updates
   the person track, and the animal is never seen at all.
2. **The occlusion spawn gate.** A candidate ≥ `occlusionContainment` (0.6)
   contained in a track matched this frame never spawns. It exists for a
   detector double-firing inside a subject it already has. A dog at the knee of a
   person whose 4K box is 600×1000 px is 100% contained in it and is not the
   person.

D377 had exempted `nativeRecovery` from a THIRD such guard — the low-score
person-over-animal spawn fold — with exactly this argument, and simply did not
reach these two. The remaining explanations were checked and are not the cause:
the pass runs at its cadence cap during a passage (above), and births do keep
happening during a traversal (07:27:00 `bestScore=0.9344`, 07:31:38, 06:41:31),
so the subject is not merely unborn — it is born, then eaten, repeatedly.

Raising `RECOVERY_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`, `MAX_CROPS_PER_FRAME` or lowering
`MIN_UNCOVERED_FRACTION` was rejected: nothing measured says the pass runs too
rarely on this camera, and each costs 60–290 ms of inference on every camera to
fix a branch that costs nothing to fix directly. No constant is changed here.

## Decision

A gate whose purpose is to doubt the COARSE full-frame pass never applies across
classes to a detection carrying `nativeRecovery`. Concretely, in `SortTracker`:
such a box associates to its OWN class or it spawns (`associable` and
`looseMatch`), and a cross-class occluder does not refuse it a birth
(`occludesSpawn`). One predicate, `sameGroupAsMeasured`, states it once.

And, in the runner, why a frame ran no pass is a COUNTER, not an argument:
`countRecoverySkips` classifies a frame's motion regions against the same
predicate `planRecoveryCrops` filters on, and the ten-second pass report carries
`skipRateLimited`, `skipNoMotion`, `skipTooLarge`, `skipCovered` beside the
passes that ran.

## Consequences

- Every OTHER spawn guard still applies to a recovered box: the per-class score
  floor, the area floor, the `dedupSpawnIou` (0.6) overlap — which is what stops
  a recovered box that really is the person's own box — the same-class occlusion
  gate, and `resolveDuplicates` afterwards, where a `person` still beats a
  cross-class `animal`. The narrowing moves the decision from a silent
  swallow at association time to a duplicate resolved on evidence.
- A `person` track can now coast beside a newly born `animal` track where before
  the animal box sustained the person. That is the intended shape: two subjects,
  two ids. The class-flip case the widening exists for is untouched, because a
  coarse-pass flip carries no `nativeRecovery`.
- The skip counters cost four integers per camera and no log lines. They are
  drained by the report line, so a camera whose pass NEVER runs reports nothing
  until it does — the counters accumulate and the `windowMs` says how long.
- Nothing here changes a threshold, a cadence or a model. The cost of the pass is
  unchanged: 60–290 ms, a third of the full-frame step (`miss-dog-101`).

## Enforcement

Tests, each confirmed to fail with its fix removed.
`__tests__/sort-tracker-native-recovery-survives.spec.ts` drives the real
geometry — a 600×1000 person box and a 250×250 dog inside it — and pairs every
assertion with a counter-test that must keep the OLD behaviour when
`nativeRecovery` is absent, plus one that a SAME-class occluder still refuses a
recovered box. `small-subject-recovery.spec.ts` pins that `countRecoverySkips`
classifies as eligible exactly the region `planRecoveryCrops` plans on, so the
report can never name a gate the planner does not apply.

## Update 2026-09-09 — the refusal has a second consumer, and it is not sampled

`reportRefusal` now writes to TWO channels. The first is the one this record
describes and has not changed: sampled (`SpawnRefusalSampler`, the first ten and
then one in ten), destined for the log, and the counters this ADR calls
"why a pass did not start is a counter" keep counting
exactly as before.

The second is new, goes to the frame path, and **is not sampled**: a
detection refused inside a watched zone still emits its event
(D422 and the session of 2026-09-09), and nine out of ten of those events
vanishing would make a notification depend on where a counter falls.

Nothing this record states about the coarse pass and the native
one has changed: the second channel observes, it does not decide.
