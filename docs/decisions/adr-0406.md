## Context

The SEMANTIC tier (2026-08-21) exists so the best shot is the frame that
PROVED something about the subject rather than the biggest anonymous view. The
comparator and the ranking are right — replayed against the real stored boxes
of track `718acf29` the plate frame beats the held peak — but the tier was
almost unreachable, for two independent reasons, and both are gates on
something OTHER than the recognition.

**Faces were gated on IDENTITY.** `face-recognizer.ts` fired the signal only
for candidates the gallery AUTO-MATCHED. A stranger's face therefore never
raised the tier. Operator, 101 `acad3a3e` (person, 7.1 s): *"il keyframe è
preso a metà busto quando abbiamo un frame con la faccia completamente
visibile"* [the keyframe is taken at half-bust when we have a frame with the face fully visible]. The persisted record says exactly that: `hasFace`,
`hasEmbeddedFace`, a stored `faceCrop` at `…208655`, NO `recognizedIdentityId`
— and `keyFrame`/`thumbnail` both at `…212992`, 4.3 s later, on a frame that
proved nothing. Same shape on 637 `ba28ca8f`: `faceCrop` at `…460296`, no
identity, `thumbnail` frozen on the birth frame at `…459167` while the
`keyFrame` sits at `…460296` — the operator's *"best shot e key frame sono
diversi"* [best shot and key frame are different].

**Plates were gated on the LABEL WRITE.** The promotion sat behind
`applied.trackWritten`, i.e. behind `resolveTieredLabelWrite` letting the OCR
text into a slot against every earlier candidate. 102 `718acf29`: plate DNJE230
read and attributed at `…992717` (`subLabelMeta.decidedAt`), best shot stored
from `…987020` and keyFrame from `…987158` — 5.6 s before the plate.
*"Il frame al momento della targa è decisamente migliore come best shot."* [The frame at the moment of the plate is decidedly better as the best shot.]

What was NOT changed, deliberately: the comparator, the tier order (square
containment > semantic > edge), the hysteresis, the rate limit, and
`recognitionMinFacePx`. No new threshold was introduced (D222).

## Decision

A frame earns the semantic tier the moment the RECOGNITION succeeded on it,
never on a downstream decision about what the record kept.

- **Face** — `onSemanticFace` fires for every candidate that cleared
  `recognitionMinFacePx` and produced an embedding. The identity rides along
  when there is one (`kind: 'face'`) and is absent when there is not
  (`kind: 'face-visible'`); it is never faked. A face BELOW the recognition
  floor still fires nothing — that floor stays the bar.
- **Plate** — the promotion moves to the READ, beside `observePlateRead`.
- **Fine classification** — keeps the label-write gate: a class the track
  REFUSED is not something the record keeps, and it must not steer the shot.

Which of the two moments applies is `detailSemanticTrigger(className)` in
`pipeline/detail-semantic-gate.ts` — one total function consulted at both call
sites, so no detail can promote twice and none can promote never.

A promotion that does NOT name the subject (`origin: 'evidence'`) raises the
best-shot tier and nothing else: `recognizedTrackIds`, which answers the
group-shot scorer's "was this subject recognised?", stays behind the identity.

## Consequences

- The semantic tier is now reachable on most person traffic, not only on
  enrolled faces. Within the tier the ranking is unchanged, so among face
  frames the biggest, best-contained one still wins — a face frame simply can
  no longer lose to a face-less one on size.
- More promotions means more re-fired captures. They are bounded exactly as
  before: the first semantic frame is a tier upgrade (bypasses `minGapMs`),
  every later one pays the 0.05 hysteresis and the 2 s gap, and
  `captureSemanticBestShot` still skips a thumbnail whose capture is in flight.
- `best-shot: semantic promotion` lines now carry `kind: 'face-visible'`. A
  query counting recognitions by that line must filter on `kind`.
- Adding a new detail class that IS its own recognition means adding it to
  `detailSemanticTrigger`, not to a call site.

## Consequences NOT covered

The `keyFrame` and the `thumbnail` are still two independently scheduled
captures for one decision, and the keyFrame additionally has a bounded RETRY
path (`key-frame-retry.ts`) that captures whatever frame is CURRENT when a
native fetch finally succeeds. So the two artefacts can still disagree on a
track that never raises a semantic frame and whose keyFrame landed on a retry.
That divergence is deliberate — the retry exists because a peak-only gate left
71.6% of miss-logged tracks with no keyFrame at all — and it is named here
rather than left to be rediscovered.

## Enforcement

`__tests__/face-recognizer-semantic.spec.ts` pins all four face cases
(auto-match, suggestion band, empty gallery, below the recognition floor);
`pipeline/__tests__/detail-semantic-gate.spec.ts` pins the plate/classification
split and its exhaustiveness; `summary/__tests__/summary-wiring.spec.ts` pins
that the group-shot "recognised" mark stays behind `origin === 'identified'`.
Neutralising either fix turns the first two red. The two `index.ts` call sites
are review-only beyond the wiring assertion.
