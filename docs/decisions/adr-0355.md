## Context

`deriveBatteryPresence` (D173) calls a sleeping battery camera `unreachable`
after six hours without a passive contact — a push, or a poll the camera
answered while awake. That works for a hub child, whose contact the hub
refreshes. A STANDALONE camera stamps contact only from inbound Baichuan
pushes, and a standalone battery camera pushes only when it wakes: on
2026-09-05 the doorbell (4263) pushed once in 30 hours of log. So every
quiet night ended with the doorbell and the baby monitor (106) painted as a
fault at about 3 AM, until the first motion — a verdict reached without
anybody having asked the camera anything.

Two alternatives were rejected. A longer silence budget only moves the false
fault later and still never asks. `probeSleepStatus` (a non-waking command
with a short timeout) cannot help: it times out identically for "asleep" and
"gone", so it produces no evidence either way.

D173's contract reads "no self-initiated path may reach `ensureApi()` while
the camera is known to be sleeping". It was written against paths that woke
cameras for nothing (a kernel probe retry chain, a constructor-time seed).
This path wakes for exactly one thing the camera cannot otherwise provide.

## Decision

In the hourly battery cycle, a STANDALONE battery camera believed asleep is
woken ONCE when the next tick would land past the presence budget
(`shouldWakeForContact`: `silence + tick >= budget`), through the shared
proactive-wake cooldown, and every slice is refreshed while it is up
(battery, cap warmers, aux devices, snapshot). A wake that answers stamps
contact and the silence restarts. A wake that fails is left alone: the
silence reaches the budget and the camera reads `unreachable` — for the first
time on evidence. Hub children are never woken this way; the hub answers for
them. The path is registered in the autonomous-wake census spec.

## Consequences

- One login per budget window per standalone camera, at most — about four a
  day, only on quiet days. A camera that pushes on its own is never woken.
- `unreachable` on a standalone camera now means "we tried and it did not
  answer", not "it was quiet".
- D173's absolute is narrowed, not abandoned: the census still rejects every
  OTHER self-initiated wake, and this one must keep its cooldown gate BEFORE
  `ensureApi()`.

## Enforcement

`battery-autonomous-wake-census.spec.ts` asserts the method exists, gates on
the cooldown before `ensureApi()`, marks the wake, and is called from the
periodic cycle only. `battery-contact-expiry.spec.ts` pins the decision.
