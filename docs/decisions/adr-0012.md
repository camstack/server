## Context

`getDeviceAggregate` and the settings/status aggregators used to enumerate
providers from `registry.listCapabilities()` and fan the per-device call out to
every provider with a matching capability name. That works while one vendor
implements a capability. It breaks the moment two do — and capability names are
shared *by design*: `stream-params` and `ptz` come from both the Reolink and the
Hikvision integrations.

The result was a Reolink camera being asked for its stream parameters by the
Hikvision provider, which answers something — plausibly shaped, wrong, and
attributed to the right device.

## Decision

Per-device aggregation walks `getBindings(deviceId)` **only**. A device belongs to
exactly one integration; its contributions come only from providers bound to it.

## Consequences

- Adding a second vendor for an existing capability is a non-event.
- Anything device-scoped that starts from a capability *name* rather than from a
  device is suspect. The direction of the lookup is the invariant.

## Enforcement

Review. The failure mode is silent and returns well-formed data, so this record is
the primary defence — worth reading before touching any aggregator.
