## Context

Everything [D126](adr-0126.md), [D133](adr-0133.md) and [D137](adr-0137.md)
added to the alarm arrived as PROSE, and each addition made the sentence longer
because each new fact was appended to the previous sentence rather than given a
line:

> Attivo tra 30 secondi — ma ogni dispositivo coperto da questa modalità è
> escluso (Porta retro), non proteggerà nulla.
>
> [Active in 30 seconds — but every device covered by this mode is excluded
> (Back door), it will protect nothing.]

> 2 dispositivi attivi: Front door, Garage. Esclusi: Porta retro.
>
> [2 devices active: Entrance, Garage. Excluded: Back door.]

> Finestra porch è aperto. Premi di nuovo entro 60s per inserire senza di esso.
>
> [Porch window is open. Press again within 60s to arm without it.]

The operator reads these on a lock screen and the question is always "WHICH
one" — which window is open, which camera is excluded. A paragraph has to be
parsed to answer that, and the `advanced-notifier` this hub's alarm was modelled
on never did: it renders `Bypassed devices: a, b` — one label, one
comma-joined list, `None` when empty.

The second half is worse, and it is the notification that matters most. A rule
that trips the alarm produced ONE row (the combined delivery, D126) whose body
was the alarm's own invention:

> L'allarme è scattato per Intrusione su Cancello
>
> [The alarm went off for Intrusion on Gate]

That is the only notification in the system that does not look like the rule's
other notifications: no identity, no zone, a subject that is the rule's NAME
rather than what was seen. The operator wakes at 3am to a sentence they have
never read before, about an incident whose familiar phrasing
("persona (John) su Cancello in gate") exists three lines away in the code.

## Decision

**An alarm body is a block of `Label: list` lines, never a sentence with a list
inside it.** The mode and the countdown live in the TITLE
(`system.alarm-arming.title.<mode>` renders `{{seconds}}`); the body carries one
line per device group — active, excluded, bypassed, open. Variant selection
stays at intake and each variant keeps its own catalog key, so the Texts tab
(D124) can re-word "nothing is armed" without touching "these are armed". The
ARMING body reports only the EXCEPTIONS: the armed list arrives thirty seconds
later in the arm announcement, and printing it twice is the verbosity this
replaced.

**A combined row is the triggering rule's own notification with a severity
prefix.** Its title is `alarm.combined.title` (shipped `Alarm: {{deviceName}}`,
overridable per-rule via `template.title` and per-key in the Texts tab) and its
body is `detection.body` — the same key, the same composites, the same
vocabulary the rule's ordinary notification would have rendered. To make that
possible `NcRuleFireRef` freezes the detection vocabulary (`className`, `label`,
`zones`, `confidence`) alongside the media owners it already carried, and the
dispatcher composes `{{subject}}` and the zone clause from the fire ref rather
than from the system event.

## Consequences

- The intake's key selection and the dispatcher's variable composition are
  gated on the SAME fact — `alarmSource.className !== undefined`. A fire ref
  without a class (a device-event match) and every row enqueued before this
  deploy keep the alarm's own frozen sentence, because `detection.body` with no
  class renders `{{subject}}` from the system event's DEDUP SLOT and delivers
  "alarm:42 su Front door".
- `{{seconds}}` is frozen in `textParams` for every `alarm-arming` transition,
  not only for the ones whose coverage could be derived. The title asks for it
  unconditionally, and a transition that composed no body would otherwise
  render "Inserimento Casa — s".
- The arm/arming bodies no longer count rules. That number was never actionable
  and it was the thing that made every variant a plural map.
- `system.alarm-triggered.body.rule` survives as a legacy key. It is produced
  only for a source with no detection vocabulary, and it is what the durable
  rows already in SQLite drain as.

## Enforcement

- `alarm/__tests__/alarm-message-shape.spec.ts` — pins the list structure per
  variant in both locales, and bounds every rendered line at 64 characters. A
  body that becomes a paragraph again fails here.
- `__tests__/alarm-combined-notification.spec.ts` — pins the intake's key
  choice, the composed body in both locales, the per-rule template and Texts-tab
  overrides winning, and that a pre-deploy row still renders from its frozen
  keys. Neutralising either the intake selection or the dispatcher composition
  turns it red.
- `alarm/__tests__/alarm-texts.spec.ts` — every key the alarm can freeze onto a
  row exists and renders non-empty in every shipped locale; `detection.body` and
  `alarm.combined.title` are listed there because a rename of either would
  silently blank the combined notification.
- `scripts/check-nc-text-catalog.ts` — locale parity, placeholder sets and
  plural categories.
