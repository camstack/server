## Context

What forced a choice. The problem, the constraint, and — most valuable — the
thing that was tried first and did not work. A record without a failure in it is
usually a record nobody needed.

## Decision

One paragraph, stated as a rule someone can follow or violate.

## Consequences

What this costs. What it forbids. What now has to be true elsewhere for it to
keep holding.

## Enforcement

How a violation is caught: a guard script, a codegen check, a test — or
"nothing, review only", which is worth writing down because it tells the next
person how much to trust the rule.
