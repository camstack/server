## Context

The Dreame robot camera (a child of the vacuum, `DeviceType.Camera`) shipped
with `navigation` only. It had never had a stream: no `stream-catalog`, no
`battery`. The stream-broker's catalog reconcile asked it every poll and logged
`no provider registered for device-native cap "stream-catalog" on device 4374`,
the device page reported the broker inactive, and the operator read both as
"the Dreame cameras do not work".

The camera cannot be wired like a mains camera. `@apocaliss92/nodedreame`
carries video only between `DreameCameraController.open()` (a cold start on
the robot plus a keep-alive loop) and `close()`; the relay URL exists only
while the monitor is open, and every open costs the robot's battery and a
cloud relay. A catalog that always advertised a URL would make the broker dial
a relay that carries nothing; a catalog built by opening the monitor at every
reconcile would wake the robot every poll.

What already existed: the broker's battery path. A play that finds NO broker
for a battery camera (`DeviceFeature.BatteryOperated`) calls
`battery.wakeForStream`, then reconciles that device's catalog and retries the
broker for a settle window (`battery-wake-on-play.ts`, D173). The wake is
DEMAND — the one moment the broker is allowed to touch a battery camera.

## Decision

The robot camera is a battery camera to the broker, and its catalog is empty
while its monitor is closed.

- The camera child registers `battery`: `sleeping` is "the monitor is
  closed", `lastContactAt` is the robot's last state change (the broker
  refuses to wake a camera silent for hours), and `wakeForStream` opens the
  monitor inside the caller's bound — an open that outlives the bound keeps
  running, and the next play joins it (single in-flight open).
- The camera child registers `stream-catalog`: one `pull-rtmp` descriptor
  carrying the relay URL the current `open()` handed out, `battery-operated`
  in its features (prebuffer off); NOTHING while the monitor is closed. The
  empty catalog is the mechanism, not a gap: it is what makes a play ask for a
  wake instead of dialling.
- The monitor closes on its own: after three consecutive quiet minutes
  (`streamBroker.listClients` for `<deviceId>/live` shows no RTSP client, no
  WebRTC/encoded subscriber, no audio listener), when the relay's keep-alive
  fails, and when the device is removed. The detection pipeline's decoded
  subscription is deliberately NOT a reason to stay open — it exists for as
  long as a stream exists, and counting it would hold the relay open until the
  battery died.

## Consequences

- First frame after a cold start is a wake away: the operator sees the
  broker's honest `sleeping`/`waking` states, never "No broker for stream".
- The relay is RTMP only in name (LinkVisual: a private handshake byte, a
  60 KB chunk size, a pipelined connect/play, its own pings) — a standard
  RTMP client, and ffprobe, are closed right after `play`. The camera child
  therefore does what the reference NVR plugin (`the reference plugin`) proved
  on this robot, and what Reolink's native streams do here: nodedreame's
  `DreameCameraStream` speaks the relay in the provider, nodelink's
  `Rfc4571Muxer` frames its access units as RTP on a local TCP server
  (`dreame-rfc4571-server.ts`), and the catalog names that server as a
  `pull-rfc4571` source with the SDP built from the first keyframe. The
  broker needs no Dreame knowledge — a first draft put a LinkVisual client
  inside the broker's RTMP reader; it streamed, and was withdrawn the same
  day because the provider owning the session is the shape everything else
  already has.
- The consumer leaving IS the close: the server tells the device when the
  broker's reader detaches (after a 4 s grace), and the device also caps a
  monitor at 10 minutes, the plugin's rule. On open the device PUBLISHES the
  stream on the broker and on close it RETRACTS it — a registered stream is
  one the next play dials instead of waking, which is how a play answered
  "no source media" against a closed relay.

## Enforcement

`dreame-camera-stream.spec.ts` pins the facts: the slice is asleep while
closed, the catalog is empty while closed and one `pull-rtmp` while open, and
the pipeline alone never counts as a viewer. The wiring is review only.
