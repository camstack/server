## Context

The confirmation gate asks one question of a new track: re-run the detector on a
crop of the box and see whether the subject is really there. It asked for those
pixels AFTERWARDS, by `FrameHandle`, across a process boundary — and the request
does not arrive.

Three hours on the live hub, 2026-09-07 06:20-09:20, 20 cameras with gate
traffic:

```
native crop miss (servedTier=miss)   7 452   reason=worker-lease-gone   7 452
                                             reason=no-registry-entry       6
handleAgeMs on miss                  p50=1 347  p90=24 161  max=67 390
registry fullness                    registrySize=4096 registryCap=4096
                                     in 6 814 of 6 849 samples (99.5%)
gate verdicts                        1 883    confirmed  637   suppressed 151
                                              timeout  1 048   no-crop    143
                                              native-pass 33   below-min-crop 4
```

**The registry cap is not the cause.** It is pinned full 99.5% of the time and
that is what the first diagnosis blamed — but capacity eviction produces
`no-registry-entry`, and there were SIX of those against 7 452. Partitioning the
node-wide registry per camera, or raising its cap, would have moved nothing. The
entry is present; the WORKER refuses.

Why it refuses is one line further down. A decode worker's own metrics:

```
leaseOffered:177, leaseAdmitted:9, leaseMarks:297,
leaseReleaseMisses:288, leaseReleasedEarly:288, leaseMb:0, leaseFrames:0,
tilesCut:7
```

288 of 297 frames that reached inference are WITHDRAWN from retention before
they are retained (`noteLeaseRelease(..., 'cut')` unmarks a frame whose cut
arrives before its supersede), so nine frames are ever leased, the tile store is
effectively empty, and every later crop of any age misses. That is why a request
`254 ms` after the handle was registered already fails: the frame is not evicted
late, it is never kept.

So **1 191 of 1 883 gate verdicts (63%) never saw a pixel** — and a birth the
gate cannot confirm produces no track, no object event, and therefore neither a
notification nor an entry in the events reel. The operator's requirement is that
those two coincide: "se ricevo una notifica di un cane su aiuola, mi aspetto di
trovarla anche nel reel eventi" [if I receive a notification about a dog on the flower bed, I expect to find it in the events reel too].

Two things were tried first and are recorded because they did not work:

1. **Sizing the retention window.** 64 → 256 (2026-07-19, against the ~1 s
   lease) → 4 096 (2026-09-05, against the 30 s tile window). The miss rate did
   not follow, because the binding constraint was never the window.
2. **Fixing the withdrawal race in the worker.** Correct, and rejected here: it
   would turn retention ON for every inference frame on every camera against a
   `budgetMb=1024` lease store that currently holds `leaseMb:0`, i.e. it buys the
   gate's pixels with an unmeasured amount of RAM on a hub already at ~80%. This
   repo has already written down that memory ceilings are patches. It remains the
   right fix for the FULL-FRAME consumers (`keyFrame`, detail `frameJpeg`) and is
   named here so the next session does not rediscover it.

The reference NVR, asked the same question, does not have this problem because it makes
the after-the-fact path structurally impossible. Its decoded frame is invalidated
the instant the `for await` body returns (`plugins/python-codecs/src/libav.py:105-112`,
`vipsimage.py:20-24`), the generator queue is depth 1 and drop-on-full
(`libav.py:37,52-56`; objectdetector passes `queue: 0` at
`plugins/objectdetector/src/main.ts:372`), so every second look — face crop, CLIP
embedding, retained detection image — is taken IN BAND, inside that window
(`plugins/openvino/src/predict/face_recognize.py:61-75`,
`plugins/objectdetector/src/main.ts:512-520`). Its only after-the-fact store is a
PER-CAMERA map of JPEGs with a 10 s timer and no capacity cap
(`plugins/objectdetector/src/main.ts:53`, `650-661`), populated eagerly one JPEG
per detection, and every consumer is written around
`'Detection not found. It may have expired.'` (`main.ts:677-683`).

## Decision

**A candidate birth carries the pixels it was measured on.** The runner cuts a
small JPEG of the box, padded, from the frame its own result callback already
holds — the process-local lease first (`localFrameRegistry`), the inline raw
buffer otherwise — and it rides the detection as `ObjectDetection.birthEvidence`.
The confirmation gate looks at that crop BEFORE asking anyone for one. Only a box
the camera has NOT been showing gets a cut (`BirthNoveltyMemory`, per camera, per
class), so the encode is paid per birth and not per frame.

**The evidence is held in a RESERVE that belongs to one camera.** A deferred
birth is re-offered on later frames where the same track carries nothing, so
post-analysis keeps the crop in `BirthEvidenceStore`:
`BIRTH_EVIDENCE_SLOTS_PER_CAMERA` slots per camera, evicted by AGE inside that
camera's own quota and never across cameras. No shared cap is raised; the one
that existed is simply no longer on this path.

**Only a NATIVE crop of the birth instant may suppress. Every other crop may
confirm and may never suppress.** Two sources are not that, for two different
MEASURED reasons:

- `fallback` cuts from a LATER frame. Finding the subject there proves it was
  here; not finding it proves only that it is not there now — a walking dog is a
  metre away and its box holds grass. That outcome is `undecided` with reason
  `fallback-inconclusive`.
- `carried` is cut from the ≤640 ANALYSIS raster, so a small subject there is
  exactly D377's "third, different, worse image". The first version of this
  change let it suppress, shipped, and was measured on the live hub within twenty
  minutes: **the carried crop suppressed 133 of 192 births (69%) while the NATIVE
  crop of the same gate suppressed 5 of 56 (9%)**. That is not a better gate, it
  is a worse camera. So a negative carried verdict is `carried-inconclusive` and
  ESCALATES to the native path, which decides as it always did.

The rule this leaves is MONOTONE: the change can turn a `timeout` or a `no-crop`
into a `confirmed`, and it can never create a suppression — so nothing the gate
used to catch stops being caught. `cropSource` on the decision says which of the
three decided, and it is how the before/after below is read.

This is NOT a detail/CLIP crop and shares nothing with `deriveDetailCropRect`
(D52): the rectangle is a detection box padded for a CLASSIFICATION question, fed
to a re-detector and to nothing else — never embedded, never stored as media,
never indexed. It is also not D372's recovery crop, which starts from a motion
region before any subject exists. It is the generalisation of D377: that record
carried a native pass's VERDICT with the detection; this one carries the PIXELS,
for every candidate birth rather than only the recovered ones.

## Consequences

- **Measured on the live hub — and the aggregate is NOT yet proof.** BEFORE:
  2026-09-07 06:25-09:15, 1 967 gate verdicts. AFTER: 10:05-10:57, entirely
  post-deploy, 257 verdicts.

  | reason | before (170 min) | after (52 min) |
  | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | `confirmed` | 31.4% | **41.6%** |
  | `suppressed` | 7.1% | 19.8% |
  | `native-pass` | 1.6% | 3.9% |
  | `no-crop` | 7.0% | 12.1% |
  | `timeout` | **52.7%** | **22.6%** |
  | **never saw a pixel** | **59.7%** | **34.6%** |
  | **measured** | **40.1%** | **65.4%** |

  That table on its own would be an overclaim, and the before data says why. Slid
  in 52-minute windows, the BEFORE `measured` share ranges **29.5% to 88.2%,
  median 46.5%**, and it tracks LOAD almost perfectly: 88.2% at 2.3 gate
  verdicts/min, 43.8% at 7.7/min, 30.2% at 19.3/min. The after window sits at
  4.9/min, where interpolating the before curve already predicts ~64%. So 65.4%
  is ON the before curve, not above it — **this window does not demonstrate the
  aggregate improvement, and a load-matched observation (the same hours of the
  next day) is what would.** Recorded as an open item rather than dressed up: a
  green field metric is no more proof than a green suite.

  What IS load-independent, and IS verified:
  - the carried crop reaches the gate and decides — `cropSource=carried` on real
    verdicts, and `skippedNoPixels=0` on every camera after the lease fix;
  - **every suppression in the after window carried `cropSource=native`**, so the
    confirm-only rule holds in production and not only in tests;
  - `timeout` more than halved as a share (52.7% → 22.6%) while `no-crop` rose
    (7.0% → 12.1%): a candidate whose carried crop was inconclusive now reaches
    `fetchCrop` and gets an honest miss where it used to have its request
    abandoned. That is a better diagnosis, not a fix, and it is why the two must
    always be read together;
  - the change is MONOTONE by construction and by test, so the risk of it making
    anything worse is bounded regardless of what the next window says.

- **Every number here has its measurement beside it.**
  `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_MAX_PER_FRAME` = 2 — the busiest camera measured 2.0
  births/min. `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_SLOTS_PER_CAMERA` = 8 — the busiest camera
  (`hall`, 369 gate verdicts in 3 h) held at most FIVE distinct birth
  candidates in any 4 s window, the next four. `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_TTL_MS` = 4 000 —
  twice `confirmationGateMaxDeferralMs` (2 000 default), which is the longest a
  birth can still be re-offered. `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_NOVELTY_WINDOW_MS` = 3 000 —
  the same deferral budget plus a margin. `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_PADDING` = 0.15 and
  `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_MAX_WIDTH` = 320 equal `CONFIRMATION_CROP_PADDING` /
  `CONFIRMATION_CROP_MAX_WIDTH` on purpose: the gate maps a promoted secondary
  back into the frame through the crop rectangle, and two derivations that
  disagree put a box where the pixels never were. `BIRTH_EVIDENCE_MIN_PX` = 128,
  deliberately NOT the gate's `minCropPx` (48): that floor answers "is the box
  worth a verdict", this one answers "is this crop a MEASUREMENT", and 128 is the
  ~130 px blob D377 measured as a coin flip. Below it a carried crop would spend
  an inference and escalate anyway.
- **The declared budget, and what it actually costs.** ~15 kB per crop x 8 slots
  x 20 cameras with gate traffic = ~2.4 MB of post-analysis RAM, checkable
  through `BirthEvidenceStore.totalBytes()`. On the runner, one encode per birth,
  reported per camera by `birth evidence carried` (cut / failed /
  skippedNoPixels / kb / encodeMs), which drains its counters so a camera with no
  births says nothing. Measured live: **11-47 kB and 19-153 ms per crop, at
  `cut=1` per camera per ten-minute window** — a handful of encodes per hour
  across the cluster, three orders of magnitude below the budget. No memory
  ceiling is raised anywhere.
- **`skippedNoPixels` is why the crop is cut from the LEASE first.** The first
  deploy read `DecodedFrame.data`, which is deliberately EMPTY on the co-located
  path, and reported `skippedNoPixels` on roughly half the cluster's cameras.
  `localFrameRegistry.acquire(frameRef)` — the same process-local lease the
  recovery pass resolves its views from — serves both paths and took it to zero.
- **D9/D18 hold.** One ≤320 px JPEG per birth crosses the boundary; a frame at
  frame rate still does not. The runner awaits the encode before the emit, which
  is the only ordering that lets the crop ride the result — 6 ms on the frames
  that cut, nothing on the rest.
- **The static-phantom defence moves layer, it does not disappear.** The
  fallback used to suppress the brick wall camera 636 read as a `vehicle`. A wall
  does not move between two frames, so it closes short-and-motionless over and
  over and the phantom-cell memory (`minConfidenceFor`, D-phantom-cells) raises
  its bar — which is the mechanism actually shaped for a static box. What the
  fallback can no longer do is kill a MOVING subject for having moved.
- **`@camstack/types` gains `BirthEvidence`** on `ObjectDetection` and
  `SpatialDetection`. Both are type-level plus one plain property on the wire, so
  **no `@camstack/server` publish is required** — same shape as D377. The runner
  and post-analysis must be deployed together or the field is simply absent and
  the gate behaves exactly as before.
- **The withdrawal race is still there** for the full-frame consumers. `keyFrame`
  and detail `frameJpeg` still ask by handle and still miss (2 211 hits of
  `both the native lease and the retained-frame window have closed` in three
  hours). Nothing here fixes that, and this record exists partly so the next
  session starts from `leaseReleasedEarly:288/297` instead of from the registry
  cap again.

## Enforcement

Tests, each confirmed to fail with its fix removed.

- `packages/addon-pipeline/src/pipeline-runner/__tests__/birth-evidence.spec.ts`
  pins the geometry, the per-frame budget, the class-aware novelty (the D378
  600×1000-person / 250×250-dog geometry) and that a box refused by the budget is
  still remembered — otherwise the cap turns into a permanent re-cut.
- `packages/addon-post-analysis/src/pipeline-analytics/__tests__/confirmation-gate-carried-evidence.spec.ts`
  pins that the carried crop is used before any fetch and decides in BOTH
  directions, and that a fallback crop may confirm and may not suppress —
  paired with counter-tests that a NATIVE crop still suppresses (the change must
  not disarm the gate), that an empty carried crop ESCALATES rather than decides,
  and that a `redetect-error` on the carried crop does not spend a second
  inference.
- `packages/addon-post-analysis/src/pipeline-analytics/pipeline/__tests__/birth-evidence-store.spec.ts`
  pins the per-camera reserve: turning it back into a shared cap must fail the
  test that a busy camera cannot evict a quiet one's proof.

Counter-proofs run 2026-09-07: not consulting the carried crop failed 1 test,
removing the confirm-only asymmetry failed 3, replacing the per-camera reserve
with a shared cap failed 2. The D52 boundary is review-only —
`deriveDetailCropRect` is not reachable from `birth-evidence.ts`.
