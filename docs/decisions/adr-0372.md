## Context

Camera 101, a person walking a dog. The first-level detector sees a 3840×2160
frame at its 320 px input — a 12× reduction, in which a dog the reference NVR boxed at
89–277 px is 7–38 px and is not emitted. Six instants with the dog in the
scene: five produced no `animal` at all.

The obvious first move — lower the detector's confidence threshold — was
**measured and does not work**. `docs/benchmarks/pipeline-frame-model-eval.md`,
case `miss-dog-101`: at a 0.10 floor, `yolov9m-320` full-frame reports NOTHING
animal-class on two of the three frames. The object is not under the bar; it is
absent from the output. Nor does a bigger model fix it: `yolov9m-640`
full-frame is empty on the same frames. The SAME 320 model on a 640–800 px
native square around the moving region scores the dog 0.35–0.90 in 20–30 ms —
a third of the full-frame pass's cost, because the input is smaller.

The second wrong diagnosis was that the defect is only "the subject is never
born". On 2026-09-06 19:54:33 the gate DID confirm an `animal` birth on 101
(`bestScore 0.8983`). That track held 9 positions over 1 666 ms, coasted out
its `maxMissedMs` and died at 19:54:39 — both its object events carry
`zones: []`, while the reference NVR, in the same seconds, notified the animal at
x≈3200 INSIDE the Aiuola polygon. The subject was born, then lost, then in the
zone with nobody watching. A recovery gated on "the full-frame pass produced no
detections" would not have run on one of those frames: the PERSON was still
detected throughout.

Separately, the confirmation gate had been MEASURING the missing subject all
along. It re-runs the same detector on the native crop of every birth, and on
101 it found the dog at 0.74–0.91 inside the PERSON's crop, five times — and
wrote the number to a log line (`bestIncompatibleScore`) that nothing consumed.

## Decision

A second detector pass runs at NATIVE resolution on the motion regions the
full-frame pass does not account for, and the trigger is the uncovered motion
region — never "the frame produced no detections". A subject the crop pass or
the confirmation gate MEASURES becomes a detection in frame space before the
tracker, fused by class-aware NMS that only ever ADDS to the full-frame result.
Thresholds are not lowered to compensate for scale.

## Consequences

- The recovery crop derivation is a THIRD derivation, distinct from
  `deriveDetailCropRect` (D52): it starts from a motion region, before any
  subject exists, and its output is a detection, never a vector. Nothing may
  reuse it for a detail/CLIP crop and it must never call the detail derivation.
- The pass costs up to `RECOVERY_MAX_CROPS_PER_FRAME` (2) extra inferences per
  camera per `RECOVERY_MIN_INTERVAL_MS` (500 ms), on ≤640 px views — cheaper
  each than the full-frame pass. That cadence is floored by the tracker: at 2/s
  a subject the full pass has lost keeps its track alive with 8× the
  `maxMissedMs` (4 000 ms) margin and reaches `minHits` in 1.5 s.
- The crop is dispatched WITHOUT `deviceId`: the executor's zone gate would
  otherwise run on crop coordinates. The gating stays in frame space
  downstream, and the crop pass runs on node defaults rather than a per-device
  threshold override.
- A promoted secondary subject rides `SECONDARY_SUBJECT_MAX_RIDES` (3)
  consecutive frames — the tracker's default `minHits`, the fewest that can
  birth a track — and stops the moment a real detection of its class covers it.
  A single ride would be a mechanism that reads as implemented and does nothing.
- `SortTracker`'s spawn-dedup growth clause now applies only to SAME-class
  pairs, as its own docblock always said ("same-class partial crop"). Across the
  {person, animal} dedup pair it was claiming that a small box beside a young
  person IS that person — which is the exact shape of a dog on a lead, and which
  refused every promoted secondary. The cross-class pair keeps the
  `dedupSpawnIou` overlap guard, the low-score animal-over-person rule, the
  occlusion-containment spawn gate and `resolveDuplicates`.

## Enforcement

Tests, not a guard. `small-subject-recovery.spec.ts` pins the geometry and the
budget; `small-subject-recovery-dispatch.spec.ts` pins the wiring, including
the 101 track-lost shape (a detection elsewhere in the frame does NOT stop the
pass); `secondary-subjects.spec.ts` pins the queue's ride budget; and
`secondary-subject-e2e.spec.ts` drives the whole promotion through the booted
addon and fails if the animal never becomes a track. The D52 boundary is
review-only — `deriveDetailCropRect` is not reachable from either new module.
