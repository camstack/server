# The dog we did not report — camera 101, 2026-09-08 08:51

A dog on a lead walked through camera 101's frame. The reference NVR notified; we did
not. The complaint, as it reached this investigation, was that **we were
attached and analysing for 33 seconds and reported nothing**, on a subject the
native-crop recovery pass then scored 0.924 at the first attempt.

The 33 seconds are not what happened. **For 30.4 of them the dog was not in the
picture** — measured, not inferred: over 166 consecutive analysed instants the
production detector returns no `animal` box at any score and the motion step
produces no connected component of any size. The real defect is **1.5 seconds
wide**, and 1.5 seconds is exactly what the notification cost, because the
window in which this dog overlapped `Aiuola` was **200 ms**.

All times are local (UTC+2). 101's own OSD clock runs ≈66 s behind the
recording epoch, confirmed here on a contact sheet: the operator's photo stamped
OSD 08:50:30 is the recording frame **08:51:36**.

## Method

`scripts/motion-crop-recall-eval.mts run`, unchanged except that it now carries
each box's `bbox` through into the record (the zone arithmetic needs it) and
accepts `MCE_ANALYSIS_W`. Motion regions come from
`scripts/motion-region-area-eval.mts scan` plus the real
`packages/addon-pipeline/src/motion-wasm/region-grouping.ts`, so both the
shipped per-region bar and the merged-relative rule are the production
functions, not a re-implementation.

**218 instants, 218 detector calls, 0 skips, 0 errors**, over
08:51:05.000–08:51:40.500: 250 ms to 08:51:28.000, then **100 ms** — the
production `detectionFps` — through the decisive stretch. Pixels are
full-resolution `recording.readGopBytes` fragments. The model id was pinned and
verified against the node's echo on every call, so no number here can have come
from a substituted model. Runs were serial (`motion-crop-recall.md`'s shed
trap).

Zone overlap is computed the way `zoneEngine.annotateDetection` computes it —
**intersection ÷ the DETECTION's own area** — against the live polygons from
`zones.listZones({deviceId:101})`. The implementation reproduces the hub's own
logged figure for the one box the hub scored (3.1% on `Uscio`), which is the
check on it.

## Which world we are in

**World 2 for 30.4 seconds, world 1 for the last 1.5.**

The premise that the detector was handed 33 seconds of dog and reported nothing
is wrong. From 08:51:05.000 to 08:51:35.300 — 166 analysed instants, on two
independent node/model configurations — the full-frame pass returns **no
`animal` box at any score**, and there is **no motion component of any size** to
give a second pass anything to cut. The scene was empty; the contact sheet shows
it, and both measurements agree with the picture. Nothing was dropped, nothing
was shed (`dropped=0` on all three `pipeline stats` windows), and no filter
suppressed anything. The onboard VMD that opened the session at 08:51:05 was
firing on something at the far end of the road that no 320-input model resolves.

From 08:51:35.4 the answer flips. The dog is present, the crop pass finds it at
0.908 on the first frame a 490-px bar would admit, the full-frame pass finds it
at 0.646 from 08:51:36.800 and on 11 of the next 12 frames — **and production
reported nothing until 08:51:38.338**. That 1.5 s is a real gap on frames the
detector demonstrably could see, and it is the whole cost.

**The mechanism that fits every observation is the `uncovered-region` recipe's
own gate.** `classifyRegion` refuses a region that is less than
`RECOVERY_MIN_UNCOVERED_FRACTION` (0.35) outside the existing full-frame boxes.
The full-frame pass had a surviving `person` box from 08:51:36.700 and a
surviving `animal` box from 08:51:36.800 — both sitting on the one motion region
the dog and its owner shared — so every recovery opportunity between 08:51:36.6
and 08:51:37.9 classifies as `covered`. At 08:51:38.0 the full-frame `animal`
disappears for good (0 of the 25 frames after it), the region becomes uncovered,
and the pass runs on the very next opportunity and recovers at 0.924. The gate
did exactly what D378 designed it to do, and it was wrong here for the reason
that recipe cannot see: **the region held two subjects and the full-frame pass
had only found one of them.**

This hypothesis is not new — `countRecoverySkips`'s own docblock names it, on
this camera: *"'the second pass stops running when a person is beside the dog' —
a plausible reading of `RECOVERY_MAX_REGION_FRAME_FRACTION` and
`RECOVERY_MIN_UNCOVERED_FRACTION`, and a live hypothesis on camera 101"*. D378
added `skipCovered` to settle it. This episode is the first measured instance in
which the geometry is known frame by frame and the outcome is a missed
notification.

This is the one claim the record cannot close, and it is worth saying why:
`small-subject recovery: pass report` carries the `skipCovered` counter that
would prove it, and **no pass report was emitted for the 08:51 window at all**.
The reports either side of it show `skipCovered` running at 11–36 per window on
this camera, so the mechanism is live and frequent; it is only this instance
that is unwitnessed.

The second unclosed question is smaller and belongs to the harness, not the
system — see the first Limit.

## What motion does and does not gate on a camera configured like 101

Written out because two different answers have now been given to the operator,
and both are half right. **The confusion is that "motion" is two different
signals on this camera**, and only one of them is what `motionSources` talks
about.

| signal | what it is | who produces it | persisted? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **onboard VMD** | the camera's own firmware motion, no regions | `reolink-camera.ts`, as `MotionOnMotionChanged{source:'onboard', detected:true}` | yes — `getMotionEvents` rows, `regionCount: 0, frameWidth: 0` |
| **our WASM analyzer** | frame-diff regions on the motion view | `motion-wasm`, via `runMotionAnalysis` | **no** — lives only in an in-memory 2 s mirror |

On 101 (`motionSources: []`, `onboardMotionDrivesAnalyzer: true`,
`detectionMode: on-motion`) the onboard edge:

1. **dispatches the detection session.** `decideSessionAction` branches only on
   `detectionMode`, `detected` and whether a standing attach exists — it never
   reads `motionSources`. Verified in the ring: `detection session: dispatched on
   motion {reason=capacity}` at 08:51:05, `sinceMotionMs=28`. `reason` here is
   the load balancer's, not motion's: `capacity` means "no operator pin, the
   balancer chose the node"; the only other value this line can print is
   `manual`.
2. **triggers the recorder.** `subscribeEventCapture` filters on `data.detected`
   and nothing else, so a band with `triggers.motion` records off the same edge.
   (Not visible on 101, which records `continuous`.)
3. **opens our WASM analyzer, in bursts.** `handleOnboardMotionAnalyzer` runs
   *above* the `motionSources` gate; `shouldStartOnboardAnalyzer` returns true
   precisely because `motionSources` does not contain `analyzer`. In the ring:
   `session-decode: motion rides the detection worker (shared — no 2nd fork)
   {motionFps=4}` and then `(timing) motion stats {frames=16/30/29}` — the
   analyzer ran throughout. Its regions are the **only** input the small-subject
   recovery pass has, and they exist only inside these bursts.
4. **does not advance the runner-local phase machine.** `reportMotion` is gated
   on `attachment.config.motionSources.includes(source)`, so with `[]` no source
   ever advances it. This is the grain of truth in "motion triggers nothing" —
   and it is irrelevant twice over, because the orchestrator owns the on-motion
   lifecycle now and the session attach it builds sets
   `detectionMode: 'always-on'` anyway (visible in the `attachCamera` line
   above).

**Plainly: on 101, motion gates the session, the recorder and the crop pass. It
does not gate the runner's phase machine, and `motionSources: []` is exactly and
only that last statement.** The camera also holds no idle placement, because
`ensureMotionWatch` attaches a watch only when `motionSources` includes
`analyzer` — which is why 101 is `detectionNodeId: null` between sessions and
why every session is placed afresh.

## Two things worth fixing that this episode exposed on its own

**The zone bar the API reports is not the zone bar that runs.**
`addonSettings.getGlobalSettings({addonId:'pipeline-analytics'})` answers
`zoneMembershipMinOverlap = 0`; the running addon logged `minOverlap=0.1` on
every stamped event in the ring, including one at 09:13 after a restart. This is
the store split `object-detection.md` already documents, in the opposite
direction — and it matters here, because at a bar of 0 the 08:51:39.836 box
would have stamped `Uscio` at 3.1% and `Persona su Uscio` is a live rule. An
operator cannot currently read the bar their camera is running.

**0.1 is too high a bar for this camera's `Aiuola`.** Independently of
everything above, the same camera at **09:13:15** produced
`zone membership REJECTED by the bar {className=animal minOverlap=0.1 stamped=0
zones=[{name:"Aiuola", overlapPct:9.4}]}` — a second animal, 0.6 points under
the bar. Two `Aiuola` animal misses in 22 minutes, one of them by geometry
rather than by latency, says the bar is worth choosing from the distribution
`zone membership stamped on event` has been logging since 2026-07-31.

## The smallest change that would close the gap

**Set `motionCropDispatch: true` on 101's `object-detection` step.**

`<key>` must be a REAL inference-device key the target node reports —
`pipelineOrchestrator.getNodeInferenceDevices({ nodeId: <node> })` lists them
(`.devices[].key`, e.g. `openvino:gpu`, `openvino:npu`, `coreml:all`; this is
the same list the admin-ui's device-step-override picker reads). A made-up key
(e.g. a camera name) is refused by `setCameraStepOverride` when the node is
reachable and does not report it — writing the override under a key the
dispatcher never resolves silently orphans it.

```
pipelineOrchestrator.setCameraStepOverride {
  deviceId: 101, agentNodeId: <node>, deviceKey: <key from getNodeInferenceDevices>,
  addonId: 'object-detection', patch: { settings: { motionCropDispatch: true } }
}
```

That switches the camera from `uncovered-region` to the `motion-crop` recipe
(D396), which **has no region gates at all** — no `covered` test, no
`too-large` test — so it runs on every motion region the area rule admits. It is
per camera, it is an operator-side setting, and D396 is already in the deployed
`@camstack/addon-pipeline` 1.2.207, so **no deploy is needed**. Combined with the
`minArea: 0` 101 already carries since 08:59, the pass would arm at
**08:51:35.400** on this footage and cut a crop the model scores **0.908** on,
with the dog covering **87.1%** of `Aiuola` — 1.6 s inside the notification
window instead of 1.44 s outside it.

Its cost is measured, not assumed: `motion-crop-recall.md` collected 3 085 quiet
frames and 28 crop invocations and found **two** false positives, both night-IR
corners, and **zero** false `animal` at any gate; D396's crop-only floors
(`person 0.8`, `vehicle 0.8`, deliberately no `animal`) exist to remove exactly
those two.

Ranked below that, and each a separate piece of work:

1. **Log the recovery pass's per-frame refusal, not only its 10 s roll-up.** The
   one line that would have made this a five-minute diagnosis —
   "region R at frame T classified `covered` by box B" — does not exist, and the
   roll-up that carries `skipCovered` was not emitted for the only window that
   mattered. Same shape as the audit's fix #2: the case where the code declines
   to act is the case that is silent.
2. **Give `uncovered-region` a second opinion when the covering box is a
   different class.** A `person` box covering an animal's motion region is the
   failure mode here, and it is the one the recipe is structurally blind to.
3. **Choose `zoneMembershipMinOverlap` from the logged distribution**, and make
   the settings API report the value that is running.

## Limits — read these before quoting the numbers

- **The harness's `full` arm is not the production chain, and it matters more
  than anyone thought.** `motion-crop-recall.md` states that a 320 model on a
  16:9 camera "sees 320×180, which is what the live path detects on". 101's
  stored boxes are exact multiples of 1.2 of 3840 — integers on a **3200 px**
  raster — so the live path hands the pool a much larger picture and the pool's
  own preprocessor does the reduction. Feeding this same slice at a 1280 px view
  instead of 320 moves the first surviving `animal` from **08:51:36.800 to
  08:51:37.100** and **destroys both of the frames that overlapped `Aiuola`**.
  The true production view is larger still; it could not be measured, because a
  1920 px view exceeds `addons.custom`'s payload limit and every call returned
  `Payload Too Large`. **So the full-frame arm's first-fire time carries at
  least a 300 ms uncertainty in the pessimistic direction, and the "world 1 for
  the last 1.5 s" verdict rests on a 1.2 s gap rather than a 1.5 s one if the
  larger view is the right model.** It does not touch the crop-arm numbers,
  which are cut from full-resolution pixels exactly as the recovery pass cuts
  them, nor the 30.4 s of emptiness, where every view size agrees on zero.
- **One pass-by, one camera, one morning.** Everything here is a diagnosis of a
  single episode. The two node/model arms agreeing is a check on the
  measurement, not a second sample.
- **The motion input is a recording of the sub stream, not the sub stream.** One
  extra re-encode. `motion-region-area.md`'s reasoning applies: real motion is
  marginally more sensitive than this proxy, so the first-component times above
  are late bounds, not early ones.
- **`frame processed` proves nothing by its absence.** It is logged only when a
  track appears, is lost, or an event is emitted, so the silence between
  08:51:31 and 08:51:38 is not evidence that no frame carried a detection. It
  was checked before being relied on, and is not relied on.
- **The `Aiuola` overlaps for 08:51:35.4–08:51:36.7 come from the crop arm's
  boxes**, remapped to frame coordinates through the crop rectangle. The
  full-frame arm has no box there to compare against — that is the finding, not
  a gap in it. Where both arms have a box (08:51:36.8, 08:51:36.9) they agree to
  ~2 points (22.0 vs 19.8, 15.4 vs 13.3).
- **The zone polygons are today's.** If `Aiuola` was edited between the episode
  and this measurement, every overlap moves. Nothing in the ring suggests it was.
