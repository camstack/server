# camstack-bench-v1 — attribution

`camstack-bench-v1` is a benchmark asset built by CamStack from the works
below. It is not stock footage. The people shown did not endorse CamStack.
Each work was trimmed, scaled (and padded) to 1920×1080, converted to 15 fps,
re-encoded to H.264 / H.265 and concatenated with the others. The licence of
each part is the licence of its source; the composition is offered under
CC BY 4.0.
